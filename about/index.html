Our Mission

To promote social welfare and humanitarian support through feeding the hungry during Ramadan, providing medical care to the less privileged, and supporting orphans.

Our Vision

A society where every orphan is cared for, the sick receive attention, and no one goes hungry during Ramadan.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Showing love to the less privileged.
  • Community: Strengthening ties through outreach.
  • Transparency: Operating with integrity and accountability.
  • Orphan Support: Providing care, stability, and dignity to orphans.
