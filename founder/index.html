Our Story – The Birth of HIGOSA Foundation

The vision for HIGOSA Foundation was born in September 2020, during a period in my life when I was overwhelmed with responsibilities. Between preparing for exams, submitting multiple applications, and balancing my daily duties as a medical officer, I still couldn’t ignore a deep burden on my heart.

It started when one of our staff at Higss was rushed to the emergency unit and couldn’t afford life-saving care. One of my closest friends, who was the medical officer in charge at the emergency unit, called my attention to the situation. Together, we gathered funds and paid his medical bills. Just days later, I was heartbroken to learn that the same staff member’s wife was also hospitalized, diagnosed with cancer. Witnessing the emotional and financial strain on the family, I was deeply disturbed.

We mobilized support by spreading the word across alumni platforms, and several graduating sets began to respond with help. But I was still deeply unsettled. I couldn’t stop thinking: What if other staff members were going through similar or even worse situations in silence? That question ignited something in me.

As a proud alumna of Higss (Class of 2006), I felt a strong call to action. Despite the little free time I had, I stayed up at night, restless, determined, and hopeful. I began reaching out to alumni sets from 1996 to 2015, totaling 20 sets across both the boys’ and girls’ sections. It was not easy. With each message, phone call, and follow-up, I was planting the seeds of a collective vision. Even in the middle of exhaustion, I remained committed to making this dream a reality.

That’s how HIGOSA Foundation was born, fueled by compassion, resilience, and the power of community.

Our initial goal was simple but impactful: if each set could contribute just ₦10,000 monthly, we could start by supporting the medical needs of our school’s staff and their families. From that point forward, the Foundation has continued to grow.

Since 2021, we’ve proudly hosted annual Ramadan feeding programs, provided educational supplies such as whiteboard markers, and offered support wherever possible. What began with a single act of help has evolved into a movement of hope.

At HIGOSA Foundation, we believe in giving back to the people and place that shaped us, and extending that love beyond the school walls into the wider community.

This is just the beginning.
